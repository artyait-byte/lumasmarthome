Our studio

We build homes around the Gulf Coast hour.

LUMA is a residential technology studio based on Florida's Southwest Gulf Coast. We integrate lighting, shading, security, audio, and networking into homes that feel effortless — and we stay engaged long after turnover, because that's when most integrators disappear.

Our story

A different kind of integrator.

LUMA was founded on a simple observation: the Southwest Florida smart home market is crowded with dealer-locked systems, opaque pricing, and integrators who vanish after turnover. High-end homeowners pay for premium gear and receive a black box they can't manage, can't modify, and can't get serviced without waiting weeks for a call back.

We started LUMA to flip that model. Open platforms — Lutron, Ubiquiti, Somfy — so you own your system. Line-item proposals, so you know exactly what you're paying for. Two-hour response times. Twelve months of service bundled into every project. And a studio that stays with your home for years, not weeks.

What we believe

Five principles, non-negotiable.

Open over proprietary

Lutron, Ubiquiti, Somfy — professional platforms. No dealer lock. Your home stays yours if you ever leave us.

Network first

Every project begins with the network. Everything else fails without it.

Pricing, in lines

Every device, every labor hour. No bundled mysteries. Our margin is clear and defensible.

Design with architects

We show up early in the build, not after drywall. Integration happens in plans, not in patches.

Stay after turnover

12 months of service bundled. Quarterly check-ins. Your home keeps getting better, not worse.

Respect the hour

Technology should disappear into the day. The best system is the one you stop noticing.
