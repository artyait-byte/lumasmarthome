Security & surveillance

Your footage. Your property.

On-premise camera systems with no monthly cloud fees and no third party with a copy of your driveway. Cameras placed by walking the property, not by floor plan. Three layers — the cameras at the perimeter, the recorder in your network closet, and the alarm system that ties everything to your phone and your local responders.

A system you don't manage.

Surveillance that respects the home.

Scenarios
  1. 1Cameras

    Ubiquiti UniFi Protect G6 series: G6 Pro Bullet at the perimeter, G6 Dome at the doors, AI Theta indoors where a camera should not look like one. 4K, on-camera AI detection, PoE, no batteries.

    UniFi Protect on-prem NVR. RAID storage, 30+ days retention, encrypted at rest. Your footage stays on your property.
