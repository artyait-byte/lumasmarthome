One System, From the Gate to Your Phone

A video surveillance system is more than cameras on a wall. Behind them sit a PoE switch that powers every camera through its cable, a UniFi recorder with enough drive space for the weeks you want to keep, and a battery that keeps both running when the power flickers in a storm.

We design those parts together. The walk of the property sets the camera positions; the positions set the cable routes, the switch size and the recorder; and the plan you receive lists each of them with a fixed price.

On a home that often means a doorbell, four to eight cameras and a small recorder in a closet. On a business it can mean a plate camera at the entrance, vandal-resistant domes within reach, and separate logins for owners and staff.

Planning Coverage for the Whole Property

Start with the ways in: the drive, each door, side gates and, on the water, the dock. Those positions decide most of a system. Then add the places where something valuable sits, from a register to a boat.

Decide what each camera has to prove. Identifying a face or a plate needs the camera close or zoomed; seeing that someone crossed the yard does not. That choice sets the camera type and its resolution.

Choose how many days to keep. Retention, not the camera count alone, sets the recorder and its drives, and it is easier to size it right at the start than to add drives later.

Keep it wired. In Florida heat, PoE cameras outlast battery models and do not drop off Wi-Fi. Microphones stay off by default, because Florida requires everyone's consent before a conversation is recorded (Fla. Stat. 934.03).

Video surveillance in Sarasota & Manatee: questions we hear

What does a video surveillance system cost in Sarasota or Manatee County?

Packages start from $2,500 for 4 cameras (Home 4), from $4,200 for 8 cameras (Office 8) and from $10,900 for 12 or more (Business Pro), plus tax, with the recorder and installation included. The fixed price for your property follows the free walk.

Do you work outside the city of Sarasota?

Yes. We work across Sarasota and Manatee Counties, including Lakewood Ranch, Bradenton, Palmetto, Venice, Osprey, Longboat Key, Siesta Key and Anna Maria Island.

What is in a UniFi Protect system?

Cameras, a UniFi recorder with drives, a PoE switch that powers the cameras over their cables, and the Protect app. Doorbells, plate cameras and extra cameras record to the same recorder.

Can one system cover my home and my business?

Each property gets its own recorder, and both can appear in the same UniFi Protect app on your phone, with separate access for family and staff.

Is there a subscription?

No. Video is stored on the recorder at the property, with no per-camera or cloud fee. An alarm can be added on request; it is the only optional extra with its own terms.

Do you install video doorbells and license plate cameras?

Yes. A wired UniFi doorbell is a common addition to a home, and an AI LPR plate camera with an overview camera is the usual choice for gates and lot entrances.

Will the cameras record sound?

Not by default. Under Fla. Stat. 934.03, recording a conversation needs the consent of everyone in it, so we switch camera microphones off.

Who does the installation?

LUMA plans and sets up the system. Installation work is carried out with our Florida-licensed low-voltage partner.
