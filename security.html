Questions we get asked

What does a security camera system cost?+

Our packages start from $2,900 for 4 cameras (Home 4), from $5,900 for 8 cameras (Office 8) and from $12,900 for 12 or more (Business Pro), plus tax. The final fixed price comes after a free walk of the property.

How many cameras does a house need?+

Usually four to eight: the driveway, the front door, the back yard or lanai, and each side gate. Small offices usually need four to eight as well. We count them with you on the walk.

Is there a monthly fee?+

No. Video records to a UniFi recorder on your property. There is no subscription and no per-camera fee.

Do the cameras record audio?+

Audio is off by default. Florida requires the consent of everyone in a conversation before it is recorded (Fla. Stat. 934.03), so we leave camera microphones switched off.

Who installs the cameras?+

LUMA Smart Home, a DBA of Tegra-Media LLC, designs and sets up the system. The installation work is carried out with our Florida-licensed low-voltage partner.
