Your footage, your property.

Most residential security systems trap your cameras behind a monthly subscription and a proprietary cloud. LUMA uses UniFi Protect — footage stored locally on your Network Video Recorder, accessible only by you, with no ongoing fees.

  • On-site recording — no ongoing cloud subscriptions
