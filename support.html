How we support you

Wi-Fi maps change, cameras need cleaning and re-aiming, shade limits shift, and vendors ship updates that deserve a measured rollout. When something drifts, we realign it.

Step 01

You signal us

Call, email, or text the service line and describe what feels wrong. Photos and quick videos welcome.

Step 02

We diagnose

Remote first: we connect to the network you own, pull logs with your consent, and reproduce the issue. If it is hardware, we schedule a visit with parts on the truck.

Step 03

We close the loop

Fix, verify, and leave notes in your file so the next technician, five years from now, isn’t guessing.
