Gulf-tuned daylight

Shade that follows the sun.

Southwest Florida homes face the Gulf. Mid-afternoon sun turns west elevations into furnaces; by sunset, you want every shade up to catch the show. LUMA motorized shading is the quiet machinery that makes both happen — without you lifting a phone.

Three layers of light.

A properly shaded Gulf Coast home runs three layers on every elevation: solar (heat and glare during the day), blackout (privacy and sleep), and drapery (softness and warmth). LUMA specs all three into a single motorized scene.

  • Solar shades tuned to sun angle by time of year
  • Blackout for bedrooms, media, and afternoon naps
  • Drapery tracks motorized with Somfy Glydea or Lutron drapery motors
  • Astro-clock: open at first light, close at golden hour
  • Integration with Lutron lighting — one keypad, whole room
Scenes built around the sun

Four moments your shades already know.

07:10

Open East

Morning-facing shades rise. West stays closed to preserve coolness until the sun swings.

14:30

Shield West

Solar shades drop on west-facing glass. AC load drops. Finishes stay protected from UV.

19:40

Open All

Ten minutes before sunset, every shade lifts. The view comes back to the room.

22:45

Close Privacy

Blackout in bedrooms, drapery closes in living areas. Night mode, one press.
