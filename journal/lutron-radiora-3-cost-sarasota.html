Lutron RadioRA 3 cost in a Sarasota home

Loads, keypads, wiring and programming decide the number, not the brand. Here is how to budget and what a quote should show.

Ask three people what Lutron RadioRA 3 costs and you will get three numbers that are all correct for a different house. A Lutron system is not priced by the square foot or by the brand. It is priced by how many circuits it controls, what you touch on the wall, how hard it is to reach the wiring, and how much of the house the system is expected to run. This article walks through those drivers for a Sarasota or Manatee home, shows the ranges our own estimator uses, and lists what should be on a quote before you compare one against another.

If you are still deciding between Lutron product lines, start with choosing Lutron in a Sarasota house. This piece assumes you are leaning toward RadioRA 3 and want to understand the budget.

What you are actually paying for in a RadioRA 3 system

A RadioRA 3 job has four parts, and every honest quote shows all four.

  • Hardware: the processor, dimmers and switches, keypads, any plug-in or lamp modules, sensors, and the wallplates that finish them
  • Installation: replacing existing controls, adding boxes or wiring where a keypad needs to live, and testing every load
  • Design: deciding which circuits belong to which scenes, where keypads go, and what each button is engraved with
  • Programming: building the scenes, timeclock events, occupancy behaviour and the app, then adjusting them after you have lived with them

Hardware is the part people look up online, and it is rarely the part that surprises them. The surprises come from design and installation: a great room with eleven circuits on one wall, a keypad location with no neutral, a lanai circuit nobody can find in the panel. That is why two houses of the same size can land far apart.

The cost drivers, one by one

Number of loads. A load is one circuit you want to dim or switch: the kitchen cans, the island pendants, the under-cabinet strip, the lanai fans. Each one needs a dimmer or a switch, wired or in a panel. Load count is the single best predictor of hardware cost, and Gulf Coast houses tend to have a lot of them. Open plans with tray ceilings, cove lighting, pendants over a long island and multiple sliders to the lanai can put fifteen or more loads in what is effectively one room.

Keypads versus dimmers. A dimmer controls one load. A keypad controls scenes: Entertain, Dinner, Evening, Off. Keypads replace banks of dimmers and are what make the system feel finished, but each one is a device to buy, a location to wire and engraving to design. A house with a keypad at every entry and bedside costs more than one that keeps individual dimmers in secondary rooms and uses keypads only where people actually arrive and leave.

Wired versus wireless, new build versus existing home. RadioRA 3 is designed for retrofit: wireless dimmers and keypads replace existing devices in the same wall boxes, talking to a processor over Lutron's radio network. In a finished house this avoids opening walls, which is why it is our usual recommendation for existing homes. New construction changes the math. Pre-wiring keypad locations, landing loads where you want them and planning a panel before drywall is cheaper than retrofitting later, and it opens the door to wired and panelized options.

The processor and the size of the house. One RadioRA 3 processor covers a typical home. Large single-storey plans, concrete-block walls, detached guest suites and pool houses may need additional processors or repeaters so the radio signal reaches every device reliably. That is a site question, not a catalogue question, and it should be answered during a walkthrough.

Caséta, RadioRA 3 or HomeWorks

These three lines share a lot of thinking but are priced and scoped very differently.

  • Caséta suits a handful of rooms, a condo or a first step. It is the least expensive Lutron option, but it has a smaller device limit and fewer keypad and engraving choices
  • RadioRA 3 covers most finished single-family homes on the Gulf Coast: whole-house scenes, engraved keypads, occupancy sensors, shades on the same system, and an app that is not a toy
  • HomeWorks fits large new builds and deep remodels with a lighting designer: panelized loads, more architectural keypads, larger systems and tighter integration with fixtures

Ketra sits on top of the decision rather than beside it. Ketra fixtures and lamps change colour temperature through the day and need their own design work, so they move a project toward HomeWorks and a different budget conversation. If someone quotes Ketra on a straightforward retrofit, ask why.

Shades on the same system. Many Sarasota homes add motorized shades on the same Lutron system, so sunset drops the solar screens and warms the dining room from one keypad. That is the strongest argument for putting lighting and shades on one platform, but shades are budgeted separately. Each shade is its own motor, fabric and bracket, and wide gulf-facing openings cost more than bedroom windows. More on the layers we use on west glass on the motorized shades page.

How to budget for Lutron lighting control in Sarasota

Our budget estimator prices lighting control by how much of the house it covers. Its Gulf Coast ranges for the lighting-control line are:

  • A few rooms only: $1,400 to $3,200
  • Main areas plus exterior (most living areas, lanai, landscape lights): $3,800 to $9,500
  • Whole house plus landscape: $12,000 to $38,000

In the same estimator, motorized shades are counted per shade at $900 to $1,900 each, and the tool adds a design and project-management allowance on top of the line items. These are indicative ranges for planning, not a quote. Where a project lands inside a range depends on load count, keypad count, retrofit difficulty and how much programming the house needs.

For context, the whole-home packages on the same page put Lutron RadioRA 3 lighting in the Foundation tier, $45k to $85k for 2,500 to 4,000 sq ft, alongside cabling, Wi-Fi and cameras. The Signature tier, $120k to $220k for 4,000 to 7,500 sq ft, moves to Lutron HomeWorks lighting and shades. Those are whole-system figures, not lighting alone.

A practical way to budget is to phase it. Start with the rooms you use every evening and the exterior, which is where scenes pay off first, and leave secondary bedrooms on standard dimmers that can join later. RadioRA 3 grows well, so a first phase does not lock you out of a whole-house system.

Gulf Coast homes change the brief

Lanais. The lanai is a living room here, and it usually brings fans, downlights, pool and landscape lighting onto the system. Outdoor loads are often on separate circuits with GFCI protection, sometimes in a sub-panel, and tracing them adds installation time. Exterior keypads and devices need the right location and enclosure for heat and humidity.

Coastal homes. On Siesta Key, Longboat Key and Anna Maria Island, salt air and raised construction affect where equipment lives. The processor belongs in conditioned space above the flood line, not in a ground-level garage, and that can lengthen wire runs or add a repeater.

Seasonal owners. If the house sits empty for months, the timeclock, an Away scene that varies lights in the evening, and remote access through the app matter more than a keypad in every room. Programming those behaviours carefully, and testing them before you leave in spring, is part of the budget, not an extra.

What to ask in a Lutron quote

  • Is every load, keypad, sensor and processor listed with a price, or is it one lump sum?
  • How many loads did you count, and did you open the panel to confirm them?
  • Which rooms get keypads, and what will each button say?
  • Which devices are wireless retrofit and which need new wiring or boxes?
  • Are shades, Ketra and landscape lighting included, quoted separately or excluded?
  • How many hours of design and programming are included, and what does a change after move-in cost?
  • Who owns the programming file, and can another integrator service the system later?

A quote that answers these clearly is easier to compare than a lower number that does not. For how we approach the work locally, see Lutron lighting control in Sarasota. When you want a number for your own house, book a walkthrough and we will count the loads with you.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Lutron RadioRA 3 cost for a Sarasota home?+

It depends mostly on the number of loads, keypads and how much of the house is covered. Our budget estimator uses $1,400 to $3,200 for a few rooms, $3,800 to $9,500 for main areas plus exterior, and $12,000 to $38,000 for the whole house plus landscape. A walkthrough turns that range into a quote.

Can RadioRA 3 be installed in an existing home without opening walls?+

Usually, yes. RadioRA 3 dimmers and keypads are wireless and replace existing devices in the same wall boxes. Walls are opened only where a keypad needs a new location or a box lacks the wiring the device requires.

Is HomeWorks worth it over RadioRA 3?+

For most finished Gulf Coast homes, RadioRA 3 covers what owners need. HomeWorks makes sense in large new builds and deep remodels where a lighting designer, panelized loads or Ketra are part of the plan.

Are motorized shades included in a lighting quote?+

They can run on the same Lutron system, but they are priced separately because each shade has its own motor, fabric and hardware. Our estimator counts them at $900 to $1,900 per shade.
