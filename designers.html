Designer partnership program

Technology that follows your spec.

LUMA partners with interior designers, lighting designers, and ASID Florida professionals across the Gulf Coast. Your fabric, your fixtures, your color palette lead the project — we handle the controls, the cabling, and the commissioning behind the walls.

Decorative & architectural, coordinated

Your fixtures. Our controls.

We work downstream of your specification. Whether it's a Visual Comfort chandelier over the dining table, RH pendants in the hallway, or custom cove lighting in the millwork — LUMA makes sure every fixture dims to the right curve, colors to the right temperature, and responds to the right scene.

  • Decorative & architectural lighting under one control spec
  • Warm-dim LED tuned for evening color temperature (2700K → 1800K)
  • Shading fabric library — compatible motors for your sample selection
  • Keypad finishes matched to switch plates, hardware, and wood tones
  • Trade pricing on Lutron, Somfy, Ubiquiti, and audio partners
The designer program

How we work with your studio.

Trade pricing

Your margin, protected. We invoice the client directly or sell at trade to your studio — your choice.

Design-phase input

We review your lighting plan before it goes to the electrical engineer. Fixtures dim correctly or we flag them early.

Finish coordination

Keypad finishes and engraving matched to your switch plates, hardware, and wood tones — not picked from a catalog at install.

Model-project support

Specify LUMA on a showcase home and we'll provide photography rights and collaborative marketing.

ASID Florida member

We participate in ASID events and CEU programming, and sponsor designer showhouse events across the Gulf Coast.

Single call for issues

After install, the homeowner calls us — not you. You keep the relationship. We handle the technology.

Send us the lighting plan, or invite us to your next design review. We'll come in quiet.
