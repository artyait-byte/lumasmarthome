Smart Home Living Starts Here

The only American coast with an ocean sunset.

Our homes are oriented to the water. Mid-afternoon sun carves through west-facing glass; evening light is the reason you came here in the first place. LUMA is built to honor both — softening glare without killing the view, warming the house to the hour, and handing the sunset back to you.

The rhythm of your day

Every hour has its glow.

Your home is built around the sun — not the app. LUMA scenes rise with the light, hold the afternoon open to the Gulf, dim into the golden hour, and close the night on their own.

06:40 — First light

Alba

Shades rise to the east. Soft warm keypads light the hallway. Coffee station wakes.

13:20 — Gulf bright

Day

North-facing shades hold open to the water. South and west glare softens automatically.

19:42 — Golden hour

Sera

Lanai warms to amber. Outdoor audio drifts on. The sunset scene, on schedule.

22:30 — Goodnight

Notte

Perimeter arms. Shades drop. Path lights hold to 5% for quiet passage to the kitchen.
