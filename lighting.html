Scene, not switch.

A Lutron keypad is the antidote to the 5-gang panel of labeled rockers. Four buttons, four scenes — Morning, Dinner, Golden Hour, Goodnight — each calibrated for the exact fixtures, intensities, and color temperatures your architect intended.

  • Sunnata touch keypads in every primary room

  • Integration with shading, audio, and security for whole-home scenes
  • Occupancy + vacancy sensors in transit spaces
Scenes we design most

The seven signature scenes of a Gulf Coast home.

Wake

Bedroom indirect light to 15%, bath warm. Blackout shades rise with astro clock.

Morning

Kitchen under-cabinet + pendants 70%. Living area, 35%. Coffee station bright.

Work

Office desk at 80% cool, adjacent spaces 40%. Keeps visual focus and energy.

Dinner

Dining pendant 55%, chandelier warm, kitchen drops to 20%. The room centers.

Sunset / Sera

Lanai amber 60%, interior follows color temperature down. Outdoor audio on.

Movie

Theater goes black. Hall sconces 8%. Pathway lights remain for passage.

Goodnight

Whole house off except master path 3%. Shades drop. Security arms.

Away

Randomized interior vacation schedule. Perimeter cameras upgrade to 24/7 record.

Storm

Hurricane protocol: close shutters, kill unnecessary circuits, notify owner.
