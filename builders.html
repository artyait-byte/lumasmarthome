What changes when we join your team

Integration becomes a spec, not a surprise.

The worst moment in a custom build is when the client asks "where's the keypad going?" three weeks after drywall, and your electrician and your AV guy both shrug. LUMA enters the project at schematic design — before framing — so every outlet, cable run, keypad location, and in-ceiling speaker is on the drawings.

  • Scope review and rough-in plan before permits
  • Single point of contact for all low-voltage integration
  • Coordinated timeline with your GC, electrician, and framer
  • Final commissioning and owner walkthrough on your schedule
  • Warranty and 12 months service — handled directly with the homeowner
