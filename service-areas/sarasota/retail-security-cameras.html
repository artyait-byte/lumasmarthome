Retail Store Security Cameras in Sarasota, FL

A store needs four things from its cameras: a clear face at the door, a clear view of the register, the sales floor covered without blind aisles, and the stock room and back door recorded. We design UniFi Protect retail security camera systems for Sarasota shops and boutiques, from St. Armands and Main Street to the plazas along Tamiami Trail.

Retail losses come from three directions: shoplifting on the floor, mistakes and theft at the register, and stock leaving by the back door. A good layout covers all three without making the store feel watched. Cameras are wired PoE and record to a recorder in the back office, with no monthly fee per camera. Cameras We Install in Sarasota Stores

A typical boutique or shop uses five UniFi camera types, all on one recorder in the back office.

Over each register a dome looks down at the drawer, the screen and the customer's hands, angled away from the card reader's PIN pad. Another at the door records every face coming in.

Best for: registers and customer entrances.

A G6 180 on one wall covers the whole sales floor without blind aisles, so one camera does the work of three.

Best for: the sales floor and long display walls.

Stock rooms and back doors get a G6 Turret: easy to aim at the door, sealed against humidity, and clear in low light.

Best for: the stock room, the back door and the fitting-room corridor.

Outside, a G6 Pro Bullet records the sidewalk, the storefront and the lot, with optical zoom for faces and plates at a distance.

Best for: storefronts, sidewalks and parking.

In plazas and parking garages, cameras within reach get an IK10 metal housing that keeps recording after a knock.

Best for: exposed entrances in plazas and garages.

Store cameras in Sarasota: common questions

What does a store camera system cost?

Our packages start from $2,500 for 4 cameras, $4,200 for 8 and $10,900 for 12 or more, plus tax. The fixed price for your store follows a free walk.

How many cameras does a small retail store need?

A single-room boutique usually needs 4 to 6: the entrance, the register, one or two for the floor, and the stock room or back door.

Can the cameras record audio at the register?

They can, but Florida requires everyone's consent to record a conversation, so we leave audio off in customer areas unless you have legal advice to use it.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. Video records to a UniFi recorder in your store, with no per-camera subscription.

Can I see more than one store in the same app?

Yes. Each location has its own recorder, and the owner sees all of them in the UniFi Protect app.
