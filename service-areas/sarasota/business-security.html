Types of Business Security Cameras We Install

We standardise on Ubiquiti UniFi Protect: G6 series cameras, the G6 180 for wide spaces, G6 Pro models for exposed positions and the AI LPR for gates, all on one recorder and in one app.

Bullets are built for distance along one line. The G6 Pro Bullet adds optical zoom, so a face or a plate at the far end of a lot is still usable, and the visible housing tells people the area is recorded.

Best for: parking lots, building sides, fence lines and loading areas.

Ceiling domes cover an entrance or a counter without dominating the room. At the door they catch faces at a usable size; over a register they show the drawer, the screen and the customer's hands.

Best for: lobbies, reception desks, registers and hallways.

The all-round camera for doors and corridors: the G6 Turret records 4K, carries an IP66 weather rating and sees about 98 ft in the dark. It is easy to re-aim when a space is rearranged.

Best for: back doors, corridors, stock rooms and kitchens.

One G6 180 can replace two or three fixed cameras across an open floor or a wide storefront, with no gap in the middle. Fewer cameras also means fewer cable runs and a smaller recorder.

Best for: sales floors, dining rooms, open offices and warehouses.

Where people can reach a camera, sooner or later someone hits it. An IK10-rated dome in a metal housing survives the impact, keeps recording, and its shape gives nothing to grab or twist.

Best for: parking garages, stairwells, gates and exposed entrances.

The UniFi AI LPR reads plates where vehicles slow down, and Protect lets you search footage by plate. We pair it with an overview camera for the vehicle and driver. Plate data stays on your own recorder.

Best for: gates, lot entrances, loading yards and HOA entries.

A video doorbell at a staff or delivery door lets the team see and talk to whoever is outside before opening. It records to the same recorder as every other camera on the site.

Best for: staff entrances, delivery doors and after-hours visitors.

Choosing a Camera System for Your Business

Start from what the footage has to prove. Identifying a face at a door needs a camera mounted low, with the face filling a good part of the frame; watching a sales floor needs width, not detail. 4K where detail matters, 2K where people pass close.

Retention is usually set by someone else: an insurer, a lease or your own incident policy. About 30 days is common for offices and shops; warehouses and associations often keep longer. The recorder, from a UNVR to a UNVR G2 Pro, follows from cameras × bitrate × hours × days.

Placement has rules. At card terminals the camera sees the counter, not the PIN pad. In medical offices, doors and waiting rooms only. Restrooms and fitting rooms, never. Audio stays off: Florida requires all-party consent to record a conversation (Fla. Stat. 934.03).

Wired PoE is the standard for a business: steadier video, no batteries, nothing for staff to recharge. All UniFi hardware we install is NDAA-compliant. For a house rather than a business, see security camera installation in Sarasota.

Business camera systems in Sarasota: common questions

How much does a business security camera system cost in Sarasota?

Most offices and shops start with Office 8, from $5,900 plus tax for eight cameras. Larger sites, gates and warehouses start with Business Pro, from $12,900 plus tax for twelve or more. A small office with four cameras can start from $2,900 plus tax. The price is fixed after a free site walk.

Can I see several locations in one app?

Yes. Each location has its own UniFi recorder, and the owner sees every site in the UniFi Protect app. Managers can be limited to their own location.

Can managers have their own access?

Yes. Each person gets a login with only the cameras and history you allow, and you can remove it at any time without touching anyone else's.

How long should a business keep camera footage?

About 30 days is common for offices and shops. If your insurer, lease or policy asks for longer, we size the recorder drives for it. Clips of an incident can be exported and kept as long as you need.

Where can cameras go in a medical or dental office?

Entrances, reception, waiting areas and corridors. We keep cameras out of exam and treatment rooms and aim them so screens with patient information are not recorded.

Can a camera watch the register without recording card PINs?

Yes. Register cameras are mounted overhead and aimed at the drawer, the screen and the customer's hands, angled away from the PIN pad.

Do business cameras need a monthly subscription?

No. UniFi Protect records to a recorder in your building, with no per-camera licence. Alarm monitoring is available on request as a separate service.

Can you install outside business hours?

Yes. We can schedule the work before opening or after closing when the floor or the register cannot be disturbed.

Do you cover Bradenton and Lakewood Ranch?

Yes, all of Sarasota and Manatee Counties. See commercial security cameras in Bradenton.

Do the cameras record audio?

No, audio is off by default. Florida requires the consent of everyone in a conversation before it is recorded (Fla. Stat. 934.03).
