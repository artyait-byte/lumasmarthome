Office and business security camera systems in Sarasota

An office, a showroom or a small warehouse needs the same thing a good home system gives: clear video of the doors, the counter and the lot, recorded on site, and an app the owner can check from anywhere. We design and install UniFi Protect office security camera systems for small commercial spaces in Sarasota and Manatee County.

Every job starts with a walk of the space: entrances, the register or front desk, stock rooms, the parking lot and the back door. Cameras are wired PoE, so there are no batteries and one cable carries power and video. Recording stays on a UniFi recorder in your building, with no monthly fee per camera.

Business security camera systems start from $5,900 plus tax for eight cameras (Office 8) and from $12,900 plus tax for twelve or more (Business Pro). The packages are below; the final fixed price follows a free walk of the site.

The cameras sit on their own network segment, separate from the office computers and guest Wi-Fi. If the network itself needs work, we do that in the same project. See Wi-Fi and cabling in Sarasota. Installation work is carried out with our Florida-licensed low-voltage partner.

Home and house security cameras in Sarasota

What a business camera system covers

Entrances and exits with faces at a usable size, the point of sale or reception desk, stock and storage rooms, and the parking lot with vehicles in frame. We plan each camera for what it must capture, and we tell you where a camera would add little.

Owners and managers get their own logins with the access they need. Footage can be found by time and by person or vehicle detection, and exported as a clip when an incident needs to be shared with an insurer or the police.

How we size the recorder

Storage is a simple calculation: number of cameras, times the bitrate each records at, times the hours per day it records, times the days you need to keep. Continuous 4K on every camera fills drives fastest; recording on detection, or keeping recent days at full quality and older days at lower quality, stretches the same drives much further.

Most offices and shops ask for about 30 days. A lease, an insurer or your own policy may ask for more, so we confirm the number before choosing the recorder:

RecorderDrive baysUp to (4K cameras)
UNVR Instant16 cameras: a small office or a single shop
UNVR418 cameras: most offices, restaurants and retail
UNVR Pro724 cameras: larger sites and longer retention
UNVR G2 Pro850 cameras: multi-building sites and warehouses

The recorder goes in a locked closet or rack with the network equipment, on a battery backup so a power blip does not cost you footage. Drives run in RAID, so one failed drive does not lose the recordings.

Camera systems by type of business

The positions change with the business. We have written up the common layouts:

  • Retail store security cameras: register, fitting rooms entrance, stock room and the front door
  • Restaurant security cameras: host stand, bar, kitchen line, back door and deliveries
  • Warehouse security cameras: dock doors, yard, aisles and the perimeter
  • HOA and condo security cameras: gates, pools, mail rooms and garages
  • Medical, dental and professional offices: entrances, reception and records rooms, never treatment rooms
  • Marinas and waterfront buildings: docks and gates, with cameras mounted under cover away from direct spray

Business cameras in Sarasota: common questions

How much does a business security camera system cost in Sarasota?

Our packages start from $5,900 for 8 cameras (Office 8) and from $12,900 for 12 or more (Business Pro), plus tax. A small office with four cameras starts from $2,900. The final fixed price comes after a free walk of the site.

Do business security cameras need a monthly subscription?

Not with UniFi Protect. Video records to a recorder in your building, and Ubiquiti charges no licence or per-camera fee.

Can I watch the cameras from my phone?

Yes. Owners and managers can view live and recorded video in the UniFi Protect app, each with their own login.

How many cameras does a small office need?

Usually 4 to 8: the front door, reception, the back door, any room with equipment or records, and one or two on the parking lot. We count them on the walkthrough, not from a floor plan.

How long should a business keep security camera footage?

About 30 days is common for offices and shops. If an insurer, a lease or your own policy asks for longer, we size the recorder drives for it.

Can the cameras read licence plates?

Yes. UniFi makes a dedicated licence plate camera, the AI LPR, for a lot entrance or gate. It works best where cars slow down and the camera can be mounted at a low angle.

Do cameras survive salt air near the water?

UniFi outdoor cameras carry an IP66 weather rating. Near the Gulf or the bay we mount them under eaves or covers wherever we can and keep connections sealed.

Do you install office cameras in Bradenton and Lakewood Ranch?

Yes. We cover Sarasota and Manatee Counties. See commercial security cameras in Bradenton.

Can you also fix the office network and Wi-Fi?

Yes. Cameras depend on the network, so we can design both together: switching, Wi-Fi and a separate segment for the cameras. See UniFi networking.

Do the cameras record audio?

Audio is off by default. Florida requires the consent of everyone in a conversation before it is recorded (Fla. Stat. 934.03), so we leave camera microphones switched off.
