How many cameras a Sarasota house needs

Most house security camera systems in Sarasota come down to four to eight positions. Four covers a typical single-family home; waterfront and corner lots, or houses with a detached garage, usually need more.

  • The driveway and the street, with vehicles in frame
  • The front door and the porch, with faces at a usable size
  • The back yard, the lanai and the pool gate
  • Each side yard or gate where someone could walk in
  • The dock or the seawall on a waterfront lot

We count the cameras on the walk, not from a floor plan, and tell you where a camera would add little.

How we install house security cameras

We walk the property first, at the time of day the glare is worst, and mark each camera on a plan with what it needs to see: drive, front door, side yards, dock, pool gate. Cameras are wired PoE, so there are no batteries to charge, and they record to a UniFi Protect recorder on the property. You see everything in one app from wherever you spend the summer.

Because the cameras run on the home network, we design that too: cameras on their own network segment, the recorder on a UPS, and remote access without opening the house to the internet. See Wi-Fi and cabling in Sarasota.

Recordings stay on the recorder in your house, so there is no per-camera subscription. If you want an off-site copy, it can be added as an option. The trade-offs between cloud cameras and on-premise recording are covered in home cameras without a monthly cloud.

Security in Sarasota: common questions

How much does security camera installation cost in Sarasota?

Our packages start from $2,900 for 4 cameras, from $5,900 for 8 cameras and from $12,900 for 12 or more, plus tax. Each includes the cameras, a UniFi recorder and the installation. The final fixed price comes after a free walk of the property, once we know the cable runs and mounting points.

How many cameras do I need?

Most houses need four to eight: the driveway, the front door, the back yard or lanai, and each side gate. Waterfront homes often add one for the dock. We count them with you on the walk.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee?

No. Video records to the UniFi recorder in your house. There is no subscription and no per-camera fee.

Do you record audio?

Audio is off by default. Florida law requires the consent of everyone in a conversation before it is recorded (Fla. Stat. 934.03), so we leave camera microphones switched off.

Can I see my cameras when I am out of state?

Yes. The UniFi Protect app shows live and recorded video from anywhere, with alerts for people and vehicles.

Wired or wireless cameras for a Florida house?

Wired PoE cameras. They do not depend on batteries in the heat, the picture is steadier, and one cable carries both power and video.
