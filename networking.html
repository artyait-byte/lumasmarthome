Ubiquiti UniFi · Wi-Fi 6E / 7

Wired backbone. Mesh where it belongs.

Your house is a machine shop: stucco walls, hurricane glass, metal roofing, concrete block. Signal doesn't just pass through. We run a Cat6a structured-cabling backbone to every wireless access point, every camera, every TV, every appliance that matters — and mesh only where walking fills the gap.

  • Rack-mounted UniFi Dream Machine Pro or Enterprise Fortress
  • Wi-Fi 6E / 7 access points in every zone (coverage, not hope)
  • Cat6a structured cabling to TVs, offices, cameras, control points
  • Separate VLANs for guests, IoT, cameras, and owners
  • UPS battery backup — network survives a Florida brownout
  • Remote monitoring: we see an outage before you do
What failure looks like

The four signs of a starved network.

If any of these sound familiar, the fix is almost always the network — not the devices.

Cameras drop at peak hours

UniFi Protect recording goes intermittent between 7–10 PM when streaming spikes across the house.

Scenes fire, fixtures don't

Lutron keypad sends the command, but some fixtures respond in 3 seconds, others in 20, others never.

Wi-Fi dies on the lanai

Signal halves every time you step toward the pool. The far end of the yard has no coverage at all.

Guests can see everything

Guest Wi-Fi sits on the same network as your cameras and controls — no VLAN separation.

We can audit your existing network first.

Not ready for a full remodel? A network audit is the fastest diagnostic for most "smart home isn't working" complaints — and the first thing we do on every project.
